The Ball

Vegas has always belonged to Cleopatra. Her face is on the reels, her name is on the marquees, her gold is on every floor of the Strip. Now she has a ball of her own, and every single in the city is invited.

Cleopatra's Ball is a recurring black-tie singles event with headline music, a live royal court, Egyptian performance, and a dance floor designed for meeting someone. It began as a seasonal charity ball tour (cleopatrasball.org); cleopatrasball.com is its commercial home, built to run night after night in the entertainment capital of the world.

The Royal Court

A cast of performers and hosts runs every ball, so no one stands at the edge of the room for long.

The Queen: Cleopatra herself, played by a rotating cast. She opens the ball, rules the floor, and crowns the night.
Marc Antony: Her general and the room's boldest host. He leads the Dance of the Nile and pulls the wallflowers in.
The Handmaidens: Matchmaking hosts who work the floor all night, making introductions and running the icebreaker rounds.
The Herald: Master of ceremonies. Announces every arrival, calls every round, and keeps the night moving.

The Lotus Light

Every guest receives a lotus-shaped light to wear. When two guests who have both switched on matching are near each other and their profiles line up, both lotuses bloom gold.

Off until you say so. Matching is turned on at check-in, and off again with a tap.
Mutual only. A bloom happens only when both people have opted in.
Part of the show. Every lotus pulses with the music and flashes gold at the Midnight Coronation.
Nothing follows you home. Data stays on-site and is erased when the night ends.

Lotus Light is in development. Full privacy terms will be published before tickets go on sale.

Make It a Royal Weekend

Packages with partner resorts turn one night at the ball into three days in the palace.

Friday — The Barge on the Nile: a welcome pool party with gold daybeds, cabana tables, and an early look at who you'll see at the ball.
Saturday — The Ball: afternoon styling suite and Egyptian adornment, then the procession, the court, the headliner, and the coronation.
Sunday — The Morning After Brunch: a late, slow brunch for new pairs and old friends, with the night's coronation photos waiting at your table.
