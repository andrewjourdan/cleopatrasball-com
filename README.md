Two Temples on the Strip

Cleopatra's Ball is being developed around two of the most recognizable buildings in Las Vegas: a dome built to show the eclipse over Luxor, and a pyramid already built for a queen.

The Sphere

Prospective venue · Sphere Entertainment Co. · Home of the main event

Inside, a 16K by 16K LED screen wraps 160,000 square feet of the room and rises 240 feet overhead. For one night it becomes Egypt:

The temple at dusk. Karnak's columns and the Nile at sunset surround every guest at the Opening of the Gates.
The eclipse. Video of the August 2, 2027 total solar eclipse fills the whole dome, from the moon's first bite to the corona at totality, timed to Ra's Singles Ceremony.
The flight. Horus's falcon crosses the sky above the aerialists.
The tour. Production captured on the Cleopatra's Ball tour, from Manhattan to the Nile.

Sphere Immersive Sound, powered by HOLOPLOT. About 1,600 permanent and 300 mobile X1 Matrix Array modules carry 167,000 individually amplified speaker drivers, all hidden behind the screen. 3D audio beamforming and wave field synthesis aim sound at exact parts of the room, so the headliner can fill the dance floor while Isis's lounges stay quiet enough to talk, and Thoth's voice can seem to come from a single point in the sky. Infrasound haptics in 10,000 seats let guests feel the drums of the procession.

Outside, more than half a million square feet of LED glows gold, featuring the Eye of Horus.

The Pyramid at Luxor

Prospective venue · Luxor Hotel & Casino · Home base

The Strip's own temple, with the sphinx at the door and the beam of light at its peak. With the main event at Sphere, the pyramid becomes home base for a full night in four acts:

The pre-party. Queen's Arak and Egyptian music in the pyramid before the gates open, and an early look at who you'll meet at court.
The procession to Sphere. Guests travel together from the pyramid to Sphere for the main event of Cleopatra's Ball, carried up the Strip in a gold-lit royal procession.
The late-night show. Back at the pyramid after the coronation, the Blue Man Theater opens for a Cleopatra's Ball late show: Set's performers, Nubian drummers, Tanoura dancers, and the night's first photographs on the big screen.
The after-party. When the curtain falls, the after-party runs into the early morning for new pairs and friends with benefits alike.

Hotel room blocks inside the pyramid make every Royal Weekend a stay in the temple, a ride up the pyramid's slanted elevators from the ball to your room.

Renderings on the website are artistic concepts. Both venues are under consideration only. No venue agreement has been signed, and neither venue nor its owners endorse this event.

The Frankincense & Myrrh Ceremony

The opening rite. Before the music starts, the priesthood carries the two most precious resins of the ancient world through the hall in gold censers, the way temples of Egypt were readied for the gods.

Frankincense: the resin of the sun, burned at dawn for Ra. Bright, clean, and uplifting. It opens the night.
Myrrh: the resin of the evening, dear to Isis. Warm, deep, and sweet. It carries the night through to the end.

The ceremony will be staged to the venue's fire and air-quality rules. Scent levels are kept light for guests with sensitivities.

The Lotus

A gift from Osiris. Osiris presents every guest with the Lotus, the signature patch of the night, branded with that evening's ball. It glows, and it carries the house scent: a proprietary Egyptian blend of lotus, frankincense, myrrh, and amber.

The glow. When two guests who have both switched on matching are near each other and their preferences line up, both Lotuses bloom gold.
The scent. Fragrance only, worn on the surface. Nicotine-free and drug-free, with every ingredient published before tickets go on sale.
Off until you say so. Matching turns on at check-in and off with one tap. Prefer to skip the scent? Ask for an unscented Lotus.
Part of the show. Every Lotus pulses with the music and flashes gold at the Midnight Coronation.
Nothing follows you home. Match data stays on-site and is erased when the night ends.

The ballroom is smoke-free, so the music never has to stop. The Lotus is in development; full ingredient and privacy terms will be published before launch.

## For developers

### Local preview

Use Python 3.12 or newer for the documented commands. This static site has no package installation or build step.
From the repository root, run:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open http://localhost:8000/; stop the preview with Ctrl+C.
An internet connection is needed for externally loaded fonts.

### Validation and publishing


Canonical website: https://cleopatrasball.com/. GitHub Pages publishes main from the root; preserve CNAME.

Run `python3 scripts/check_site.py` before opening a pull request. GitHub Actions checks metadata, local links and publishing files. Also review mobile and desktop layouts and email links.

Interest registration uses email only. The former form did not send or save entries and has been removed. No precise countdown should be published until an opening date is confirmed.

Protect main with pull requests and the required Site checks job, block force pushes and deletion, and enable merged-branch cleanup. Verify Pages HTTPS, domain ownership verification, registrar transfer lock, auto-renewal, DNSSEC and mailbox delivery separately.
