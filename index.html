The Divine Court

Cleopatra and Mark Antony host the ball. Six gods of Egypt run it. Each is a performer with a role, and together they make sure nobody spends the night at the edge of the room.

Cleopatra

The Queen and host of the ball. She arrives on her litter, opens the dance floor, and crowns the night.

Mark Antony

Her general and co-host. He leads the Dance of the Nile and pulls the wallflowers in.

An Evening at Court

Nine rites from the opening of the gates to the last dance. Every one gives you a reason to cross the room.

8:00

The Opening of the Gates Thoth

The High Steward opens the doors. Guests process through a torchlit colonnade to drums and horns, each announced by name.

8:15

The Gift of the Lotus Osiris

At the threshold, Osiris places the Lotus in every guest's hand. It is the flower of rebirth, and tonight it is yours to wear.

8:30

Frankincense & Myrrh The Priesthood

The hall is blessed with the two sacred resins of Egypt. The scent sets the mood for the whole night.

9:00

The Queen's Arrival Cleopatra & Mark Antony

Cleopatra enters on her litter with Mark Antony at her side and declares the ball open.

9:30

The Singles Ceremony Ra

The dome turns from night to sunrise. Ra calls every single to the floor, and every Lotus lights at once.

10:00

The Introductions Isis

Isis and her priestesses run the matchmaking rounds: the Scarab Exchange, the Asp's Riddle, and the Barge of Strangers.

10:45

The Headliner Set

Set brings the storm: a headline set, with Egyptian musicians and whirling Tanoura dancers between songs while his photographers work the room.

11:45

The Flight of Horus Horus

Aerialists take the air above the floor as the falcon crosses the eclipse overhead. Then the Dance of the Nile.

12:00

The Midnight Coronation The Whole Court

The room goes gold. Thoth reads the night's matches from his book, and Cleopatra crowns the Antony and Cleopatra of the evening.

12:30

The Royal Lounges

New pairs and friend groups head on to after-hours lounges, reserved tables, and breakfast at partner resorts. Or call it a night. Your choice.

The Opening Rite

The Frankincense & Myrrh Ceremony

Before the music starts, the priesthood carries the two most precious resins of the ancient world through the hall in gold censers, the way temples of Egypt were readied for the gods.

Frankincense

The resin of the sun, burned at dawn for Ra. Bright, clean, and uplifting. It opens the night.

Myrrh

The resin of the evening, dear to Isis. Warm, deep, and sweet. It carries the night through to the end.

The ceremony will be staged to the venue's fire and air-quality rules. Scent levels are kept light for guests with sensitivities.

A Gift from Osiris

The Lotus

Osiris presents every guest with the Lotus, the signature patch of the night, branded with that evening's ball. It glows, and it carries the house scent: a proprietary Egyptian blend of lotus, frankincense, myrrh, and amber.

  • The glow. When two guests who have both switched on matching are near each other and their preferences line up, both Lotuses bloom gold.
  • The scent. Fragrance only, worn on the surface. Nicotine-free and drug-free, with every ingredient published before tickets go on sale.
  • Off until you say so. Matching turns on at check-in and off with one tap. Prefer to skip the scent? Ask for an unscented Lotus.
  • Part of the show. Every Lotus pulses with the music and flashes gold at the Midnight Coronation.
  • Nothing follows you home. Match data stays on-site and is erased when the night ends.

The ballroom is smoke-free, so the music never has to stop. The Lotus is in development; full ingredient and privacy terms will be published before launch.

One Ball · Every Single

Open to All Who Seek Love

Every night of Cleopatra's Ball is open to every single 21 and over, as the law of Nevada provides and as the Queen would want. There are no separate courts and no separate doors. At check-in, you tell Isis privately who you hope to meet, and she and your Lotus do the rest.

Dress Code · 100% Pure Egyptian Cotton

Dressed by Egypt

Everyone at the ball wears 100% pure Egyptian cotton. Every guest's outfit is provided by Egyptian cotton fashion brands, cut from long-staple Egyptian cotton and made in Egypt. Light enough for a warm room that dances all night.

  • For the Queens: pleated column gowns, gold collars, beaded headpieces
  • For the Generals: pleated tunics, wrapped kilts, gold cuffs and sashes
  • For everyone: one fabric, 100% Egyptian cotton, finished with your own gold
  • The Season Collection: a new gold-thread design every season for Noble tickets and above

Partner fashion houses will be announced. Sourcing and fittings take place in Cairo and Alexandria in 2027.
