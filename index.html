The Ball

Vegas Has Always Belonged to Cleopatra

Her face is on the reels, her name is on the marquees, her gold is on every floor of the Strip. Now she has a ball of her own, and every single in the city is invited.

Cleopatra's Ball is a recurring black-tie singles event with headline music, a live royal court, Egyptian performance, and a dance floor designed for meeting someone. It began as a seasonal charity ball tour; this is its commercial home, built to run night after night in the entertainment capital of the world.

An Evening at Court

From the first glass of champagne to the last dance, every hour has a reason to cross the room.

8:00

The Grand Procession

Guests enter through a torchlit colonnade to drums and horns, announced by the Herald and greeted by the Queen's handmaidens.

8:45

The Unveiling

Masks off. The court introduces its icebreaker rounds: the Scarab Exchange, the Asp's Riddle, and the Barge of Strangers.

9:30

The Queen's Arrival

Cleopatra enters on her litter with her full court, opens the ball, and names the night's first dance.

10:00

Headliner Set

A major touring artist takes the stage. Between songs, Egyptian musicians and whirling Tanoura dancers fill the floor.

11:30

Dance of the Nile

The signature partner dance. The court teaches it in two minutes; the whole room dances it together.

12:00

The Midnight Coronation

The room goes gold. One couple and one guest who lit up the night are crowned Antony and Cleopatra of the evening.

12:30

The Royal Lounges

New pairs and friend groups head on to after-hours lounges, reserved tables, and breakfast at partner resorts. Or call it a night. Your choice.

The Royal Court

A cast of performers and hosts runs every ball, so no one stands at the edge of the room for long.

The Queen

Cleopatra herself, played by a rotating cast. She opens the ball, rules the floor, and crowns the night.

Marc Antony

Her general and the room's boldest host. He leads the Dance of the Nile and pulls the wallflowers in.

The Handmaidens

Matchmaking hosts who work the floor all night, making introductions and running the icebreaker rounds.

The Herald

Master of ceremonies. Announces every arrival, calls every round, and keeps the night moving.

Wearable · Opt-In

The Lotus Light

Every guest receives a lotus-shaped light to wear. When two guests who have both switched on matching are near each other and their profiles line up, both lotuses bloom gold.

  • Off until you say so. Matching is turned on at check-in, and off again with a tap.
  • Mutual only. A bloom happens only when both people have opted in.
  • Part of the show. Every lotus pulses with the music and flashes gold at the Midnight Coronation.
  • Nothing follows you home. Data stays on-site and is erased when the night ends.

Lotus Light is in development. Full privacy terms will be published before tickets go on sale.
